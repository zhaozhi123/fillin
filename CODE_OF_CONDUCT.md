# Code of Conduct

Fillin contributors are expected to communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical issue directly.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing user data or credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact associated with the repository. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
